Where Indian Heritage Meets
A Balinese Table

Our kitchen begins before the sun does. Whole spices are toasted and ground in small batches daily, because a blend that sits loses the brightness that makes a curry worth remembering. Tandoor breads are stretched and baked to order. Nothing waits under a lamp. Whether you are gathering for a family meal or a quiet dinner for two, the table is set the same way — generously, and without hurry.

Good To Know

Do I need to book?
Walk-ins are welcome, but evenings fill quickly. We recommend booking a day ahead, especially Friday and Saturday.
How do I reserve?
Reservations are taken by WhatsApp only. Send your name, date, time, and party size — we reply during opening hours.
Do you cater for allergies?
Yes. Our kitchen handles nuts, dairy, and gluten daily, so please tell us when you reserve. We can talk you through each dish.
Is there parking?
Street parking is available along the front, and we have a small motorbike bay beside the entrance.
