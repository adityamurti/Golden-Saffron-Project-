Welcome to Golden Saffron

A royal Indian culinary experience in the heart of Bali — where spice blends are ground by hand and every curry is built slowly, from the base up.

Reserve via WhatsApp View the Menu

Step into Golden Saffron, where India's rich culinary heritage meets the tropical soul of Bali. Our restaurant seats up to 90 guests, offering an unforgotten dining experience for both casual walk-ins and reserved occasions.

We celebrate the diversity of Indian cuisine with two dedicated approaches — one entirely vegetarian and the other built around the tandoor — ensuring purity, authenticity, and exceptional quality in every dish.

Our team of expert Indian chefs, hailing from different regions across India, bring their hometown flavors and time-honored cooking techniques straight to your plate. From the spicy street foods of Mumbai to the royal dishes of Rajasthan, from the coastal curries of Kerala to the bold Punjabi tandoors — Golden Saffron is a journey through India on every table.

We warmly welcome walk-in guests and encourage you to book in advance for lunch or dinner to secure your table.

Butter Chicken
