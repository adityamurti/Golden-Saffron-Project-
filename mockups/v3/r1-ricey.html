Our Story

The idea behind Golden Saffron was born out of an enthusiasm for great food and culture.

In the Indian subcontinent, we appreciate the fruits of our nature — the patience of slow cooking, the discipline of a tandoor, the generosity of a shared table.

This is our attitude while preparing and serving our food. After years of continuous preparation and improvements, we are excited to serve you a bowl of layered, hand-ground Indian cooking in the heart of Bali.

What We Do

We invite you to join us on a culinary expedition

To taste our fresh and timely prepared ingredients, served with curries built on a base of onions, ginger, and garlic cooked down slowly — never rushed, never thickened with shortcuts.

We cook using whole spices toasted and ground in small batches each morning, leading to a result that requires little seasoning but is extraordinarily fragrant.
